How to update the website

Anyone on the board can update the MRAA website — no technical skills needed. You describe the change in plain English, a helper makes it, you check a preview, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

The five steps

  1. Sign in Go to mizzourugby.org/admin and enter the board password. (Ask Michael if you need it.)
  2. Describe your change Type what you want in the box, like you're leaving a note for a helpful volunteer. Attach photos if you have them. Click Send request.
  3. Wait a few minutes Your request appears below with Working on it. This usually takes 2–5 minutes. You can close the page and come back later — it keeps working.
  4. Check the preview When it says Ready to preview, click View preview. You'll see the whole website with your change in it. This preview is private — the real site hasn't changed yet.
  5. Decide Happy with it? Click Publish to live site — it's on the real website about a minute later. Want it different? Click Ask for changes and say what to fix. Changed your mind? Click Discard and it's like it never happened.

Writing a good request

Include the details you'd want if someone asked you to do it: what, where, when, and any exact wording that matters.

"Add a news post: the annual alumni weekend is October 10–12 in Columbia. Registration opens August 1 at 9am. Use the attached photo. Mention that Friday night is at Harpo's."
"On the Events page, change the Old Boys' Game date from September 5 to September 19."
"Add Smith & Sons Roofing as a sponsor. Their website is smithandsonsroofing.com. Logo attached."

Vague requests get a guess — "Add the golf outing on Sept 12 at Columbia Country Club, $75 per player" works better than "add the golf thing."

What the statuses mean

Working on itYour change is being made. Usually 2–5 minutes.
Updating previewYou asked for changes — the new version is being made. Usually 2–5 minutes.
Ready to previewDone! Click View preview, then publish, adjust, or discard.
PublishedIt's live on the real website.
DiscardedThrown away. The live site was never affected.

Good to know

  • You can't break the website. Nothing goes live until someone clicks Publish, and every published change can be undone.
  • Ask for changes as many times as you like. Each round updates the same preview — keep going until it's right.
  • Photos: jpg or png, up to 4 MB each. Phone photos work fine.
  • Stuck on "Working on it" for more than 10 minutes? Something hiccuped — just send the request again, or tell Michael.
  • Big changes (new pages, redesigns, anything structural) — talk to Michael first.
