Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you join the alumni network, register for events, make a donation, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, class year, and any other information you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain the Mizzou Rugby Alumni Association, communicate with you about events and updates, process donations and merchandise orders, respond to your inquiries, and improve our programs and services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
