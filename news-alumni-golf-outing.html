Alumni Golf Outing Set for September

Club News · July 4, 2026

The MRAA is hosting its first annual Alumni Golf Outing on September 12 at a course in Columbia, MO. All alumni and friends of Mizzou Rugby are welcome to join for a day on the links.

The event brings together former players and supporters of the program for a relaxed round of golf while raising funds for a great cause. Proceeds from the outing will support the current squad.

More details on registration, tee times, and sponsorship opportunities will be shared as the date approaches. Check back on the MRAA news page for updates.
