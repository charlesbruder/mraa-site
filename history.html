Championship Seasons

The trophies in the case.

1968

Heart of America Champions

The Second XV won the inaugural tournament in Overland Park — beating the 13th-ranked Kansas City Blues on the way to the title.
1974

Big Eight Champions

Missouri hosted the first Big Eight rugby tournament at Reactor Field — and won it, beating Iowa State 19–18 in the final.
1985

Omaha Tournament Champions

Missouri came home from Omaha with the tournament in the fall of 1985. Two team-mates confirmed it independently years later; no scores from the weekend have survived.
1990

Langenberg Cup Champions

The Missouri Rugby Union collegiate cup, taken in sevens overtime — Joe “Pepe” Greco remembered as landing the drop kick that won it.
1991

Kansas City Collegiate Champions

The first of three straight titles in the Kansas City collegiate tournament.
1992

Kansas City Collegiate Champions

Five wins in two days at Swope Park, closing with a 37–24 win over Kansas in the final of the eleven-team tournament.
1993

Kansas City Collegiate Champions

A third straight title, won with fifteen players and no bench — four matches without a substitution, in a tournament that turned to mud.
1997

Langenberg Cup Champions

Two shut-outs to take the cup — Rolla beaten 27–0 in the semi-final and Saint Louis University 13–0 in the final.
2012

Heart of America Champions

Undefeated through the inaugural Heart of America Conference season, sealed with a 41–10 win over Kansas State and a place in the Division 1 Sweet 16.
2017

Heart of America Champions

A conference title, a 55–17 win over Brown in the national regional, and a run to the final in Spartanburg.
2018

Heart of America 7s Champions

Five wins in a day at Lawrence for the conference sevens title — Kansas State beaten 17–12 in the final, with just 22 points conceded all tournament.
2023

Bluff City 7s Champions

Seventy-two points from three pool matches in Memphis, then the host side beaten 19–12 in the final.
