Mizzou Rugby Announces Fall 2026 Schedule

Match Report · July 5, 2026

The wait is over — the Fall 2026 schedule is set. Mizzou Rugby opens the season Saturday, September 5 at home against Oklahoma State, kicking off a nine-week slate that brings five home matches to Columbia.

The home schedule features Oklahoma State (Sept. 5), Drury (Sept. 12), Oklahoma (Sept. 26), Arkansas (Oct. 10), and Kansas (Oct. 17). The squad hits the road twice this fall — at Kansas State on October 3 and at Nebraska on October 24 — with bye weeks on September 19 and October 31.

Circle October 17 in gold: the Kansas match lands on Alumni Weekend, when the annual Alumni Match and a celebration of sixty years of Mizzou Rugby share the day with one of the sport's oldest rivalries. If you make it to one match this season, make it that one.
