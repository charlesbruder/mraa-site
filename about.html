Our Mission

The Mizzou Rugby Alumni Association exists to preserve the legacy of Mizzou Rugby, strengthen the bonds between generations of players, and ensure the future of the program through financial support, mentorship, and community.

Alumni Connection

Bringing together generations of Mizzou Rugby players through events, directories, and reunions.

Financial Support

Funding travel, equipment, and development so the current team can compete at the highest level.

Program Growth

Supporting recruiting, coaching, and infrastructure to build a stronger program for the future.
