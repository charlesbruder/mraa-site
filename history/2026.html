The 2026–27 Season

Two matches played, two shut-outs.

Missouri opened the year on August 29 with a friendly against the Columbia Outlaws, arranged as a preseason warm-up. The Tigers worked the cobwebs out of a summer layoff and won it handily, 38–0.

The season proper began a week later at Gans Fields in Columbia on September 5, and Missouri beat Oklahoma State 43–0. It was a barn burner on and off the field: the temperature hit 102 degrees with a heat index of 107. The Tigers looked strong throughout all the same — solid defence and some massive tackling — and did most of it a man short. Missouri were shown a red card late in the first half and played the last ten minutes before the break and the whole of the second half with fourteen on the field. Oklahoma State did not score against them either way.

Six trys carried the scoring: Lucas Batista and Brian Fitzmaurice took two each, with one apiece from Drew Lagner, Cullen Murphy and DJ Dillon. The pick of the afternoon was not any of the individual finishes but a multi-phase pick-and-go sequence that ran roughly ten phases — dive, set, ruck, and move the ball inches at a time until it crossed the line. A total-team score, and a fair indicator of where this side is heading.

Next up: Drury, in Columbia, on September 12.
