Mizzou Rugby Photo Archive

A Season in Pictures

The best-photographed season in the club’s history.

Thirty-two photographs survive from 2025–26 — the fifteens season through the autumn in the black and gold hoops, the sevens tournament in Kansas City in the spring, and the Old Boys match that brought the generations back together. It is far more than exists for any earlier year on this site.
