Mizzou Rugby Photo Archive

New Kit, Old Boys Day

A 65–5 win over Oklahoma State, and the alumni back on the pitch.

From Don Corwin’s posts to the Old Boys group. The club broke out new uniforms — provided by the St. Louis Bombers — against Oklahoma State on October 13, 2018 and won 65–5. Old Boys Day had been the weekend before. Can you name the players, or add a result? Tell us what you remember.

Have More from 2018–2019?

Match photos, team pictures, programmes — we want to see them.

These came out of the Mizzou Rugby Old Boys group. If you have more from this season, the form on the season page is the quickest way to get them to us.
