Memories from the 1991–1992 Season

Mizzou Rugby Photo Archive

A Shoebox Opened

Thirty-four photographs, most of them never seen outside a drawer.

Ramona Sparks Hicks posted this set to the Mizzou Rugby Old Boys group: “Finally dug out a few pics, mostly from fall 91/spring 92. If I had anything written on the back, I put it in the caption.” Sixteen of them carry names that way, and those names are reproduced below exactly as she gave them.

A note on the dating: the thread itself is not unanimous. One reply reckoned the set is “a mix of the 1990-91 and 1991-92 seasons,” and Kent Allen thought some might be earlier still, saying he was playing for the Blues in 1991. Hope Dunn dated one of them to October 1991. They are gathered here on the board’s instruction; if you can place any of them more precisely, we will move them.

Named on the Back

The Rest of the Batch

Same shoebox, nothing written on the back — though three have since been identified.

Nothing was written on the backs of these sixteen. Three have since been named by the board — Ramona Sparks Hicks, Rob Rasnic, Rob Hoens and Pat Fogarty appear above. If you recognise a face, a ground or a match in the rest, tell us and we will caption them properly.
