The 2017–18 Season

Match results recovered from the Mizzou Rugby Old Boys archive.

Missouri finished the fall half of the conference season unbeaten at 4–0. The pick of it was a 17–15 win over Iowa State in Columbia that Corwin rated “one of the best collegiate matches many of us present have ever witnessed” — Nick Romano, J.T. Schlegel and Blake Ollar scoring, and the Cyclones' last kick striking the left upright. A rare December fixture at Stillwater ended 75–0.

The spring began badly and ended in silverware. Kansas won 13–12 in Lawrence in March, mauling over a late try after Missouri had led 12–8. Six weeks later, at the Heart of America sevens championship in Lawrence, Missouri beat Nebraska, Oklahoma State and Kansas in pool play, put out Iowa State in the semi-final and took the title 17–12 over Kansas State — conceding just 22 points across the whole tournament. That earned a trip to the national sevens championship in Denver, where defending champion Lindenwood proved far too strong.
